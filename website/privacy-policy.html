Privacy Policy

Effective: November 21, 2026 · Applies to: Shui for Android (package com.shui.app)

一句话概括：你的饮水记录和设置默认只保存在本机。Play 商店版本默认关闭匿名统计，只有你在「我的 → 隐私与统计」主动开启后才会向 Google Firebase 发送不含饮水明细的匿名事件与崩溃信息；通过其他渠道安装的 APK 可能默认开启，同样可以随时关掉，且不影响任何功能。

1. 存储在本机的数据

为了提供喝水提醒与记录功能，Shui 会在你的设备本地保存以下内容：

  • 饮水记录：每一杯的时间、水量、当日目标与撤销标记；
  • 提醒设置：提醒间隔、重复日、生效时段、勿扰时段、暂停状态；
  • 功能偏好：全屏提醒或系统通知、提醒音效开关、每日目标、外观主题；
  • 提醒记录：提醒触发、确认、跳过、超时的时间与展示时长；
  • 你主动创建的 JSON 备份文件或导出的 CSV 文件（仅在你操作时生成）。

以上数据均存放在 Android 应用私有存储区（files/ 等目录）中，其他应用无法读取。开发者没有用于接收这些数据的服务器，也无法查看你的饮水记录。

2. 匿名使用统计（可随时关闭）

为了解哪些功能真正有用、并定位崩溃，应用会在「匿名使用统计」开启时，通过 Google Firebase Analytics 与 Firebase Crashlytics 发送以下信息。Play 商店版本默认关闭该开关；通过其他渠道安装的 APK 可能默认开启。无论哪种版本，关闭期间都不会发送任何分析或崩溃数据：

  • 功能使用事件：页面切换、提醒展示/打开/关闭、通知按钮点击、记水操作、引导流程是否完成、通知权限状态。记水事件只包含水量区间（≤100 / ≤200 / ≤300 / >300 ml）和当日进度区间（如 25%–50%），不包含具体毫升数、饮水时刻或单条记录内容；
  • 应用与设备信息：应用版本、Android 版本、设备厂商、ROM 家族、界面语言，以及 Firebase 自动生成的应用实例 ID；
  • 设置项的分桶值：例如提醒间隔所属区间（15 分钟内 / 15–60 分钟 / 1–6 小时等），不上报具体设置数值；
  • 崩溃与错误信息：崩溃堆栈、发生页面、设备与系统状态，由 Crashlytics 收集；
  • 大致位置：Firebase 可能根据网络请求的 IP 地址推断到城市级别。应用不申请定位权限，也不采集精确位置。

Shui 不会向分析平台发送：姓名、邮箱、电话号码或任何账号信息；通讯录、短信、通话记录、照片或文件；精确地理位置；Android 广告 ID；已安装应用列表；以及你的具体饮水量、饮水时刻或饮水明细。

In one sentence: your drink records and settings stay on your device by default. The Google Play build has anonymous analytics off by default; only after you turn it on in Me → Privacy & analytics does the app send anonymous events and crash information — never your drink details — to Google Firebase. 1. Data stored on your device

To provide hydration reminders and tracking, Shui stores the following locally on your device:

  • Drink records: time, volume, daily goal and undo flags;
  • Reminder settings: interval, repeat days, active hours, do-not-disturb window, pause state;
  • Preferences: full-screen reminder or system notification, sound on/off, daily goal, theme;
  • Reminder history: when reminders fired, were confirmed, skipped or timed out, and how long they were shown;
  • JSON backups and CSV exports — created only when you explicitly ask for them.

All of this lives in Android's app-private storage (e.g. the files/ directory). Other apps cannot read it. The developer has no server that receives this data and cannot see your drink records.

2. Anonymous analytics (can be turned off)

To learn which features are useful and to diagnose crashes, the app sends the following through Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics while "Anonymous usage analytics" is on. The Google Play build has it off by default; APKs installed from other channels may have it on by default. In either build, no analytics or crash data is sent while the setting is off:

  • Feature events: screen changes, reminders shown/opened/dismissed, notification button taps, drink logging, whether onboarding was completed, and notification permission state. Drink events contain only a volume bucket (≤100 / ≤200 / ≤300 / >300 ml) and a daily progress bucket (e.g. 25%–50%). They never include exact millilitres, drink times, or individual entries;
  • App and device info: app version, Android version, device manufacturer, ROM family, interface language, and a Firebase-generated app instance ID;
  • Bucketed settings: for example, which interval range your reminder setting falls into (within 15 minutes / 15–60 minutes / 1–6 hours). Exact setting values are not sent;
  • Crashes and errors: stack traces, the screen where an error occurred, and device/system state, collected by Crashlytics;
  • Approximate location: Firebase may derive a city-level location from the IP address of network requests. The app does not request location permission and does not collect precise location.

Shui does not send to analytics: your name, email, phone number, or any account information; contacts, SMS, call logs, photos, or files; precise location; the Android advertising ID; your installed-app list; or your exact drink volumes, drink times, or drink entries.
